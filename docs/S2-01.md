## How the flow works

```text
Shutter / gallery button (scan_screen.dart)
  → CameraCaptureService.capture() / GalleryImportService.importImage()
  → ScanFlowScreen: "Identifying album…" with progress steps
  → AppDependencies.recognition.identify(imagePath)   (MockRecognitionService)
  → result card over the camera (scan.resultCard)
```

- `AppDependencies` (`lib/app_scope.dart`) holds the services. Its default
  recognition service is `MockRecognitionService`
  (`lib/services/recognition_service.dart`), which returns sample albums
  from `lib/mock/mock_catalog.dart`.
- The mock's default script answers in turn: match, several candidates,
  not recognised, too blurry. Every screen of the scan flow can therefore
  be reached in the running app.
- The result card shows the album, its preview tracks and *Learn more*,
  which opens Album Details.
